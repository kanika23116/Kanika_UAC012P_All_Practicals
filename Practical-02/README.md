## Objective
Crop and straighten a photograph, correct brightness and color, remove at least three unwanted spots or objects, and prepare before-and-after versions.

## Tools Used
- Crop Tool
- Brightness/Contrast
- Color Balance
- Hue/Saturation
- Spot Healing Brush
- Healing Brush or Clone Stamp

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Open a photograph and duplicate the original layer.
2. Crop and straighten the image.
3. Adjust Brightness/Contrast and Color Balance/Saturation.
4. Remove at least three unwanted spots or objects using suitable retouching tools.
5. Create clear Before and After versions.
6. Save the PSD, final JPG and combined before/after JPG.
